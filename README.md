# CJ—DS / research

> Formalising discrete differential geometry and formwork mechanics for target shapes, synthesising reconfigurable, modular formwork systems, inverse design, and robotic fabrication and assembly of structural concrete geometries.

## Focus Areas

- **Geometry & Mechanics** — Discrete differential geometry for target shapes, hydrostatic casting loads, force-density form-finding, and developable strip layouts.
- **Modular Systems & Inverse Design** — Reconfigurable modular formwork, curvature- and strain-aligned patch segmentation, differentiable simulation, and surrogate learning.
- **Robotic Fabrication & Assembly** — Sequenced multi-axis toolpathing, kinematic manifold constraints, and automated assembly of structural concrete elements.

## Stack

- **Core Geometry & Simulation:** C++ (`Geometry Central`, `libigl`, `OpenVDB`, `Polyscope`), Python (`NumPy`, `SciPy`, `PyVista`, `COMPAS`)
- **Parametric & Structural Modelling:** Rhino, Grasshopper, Karamba3D, Kangaroo
